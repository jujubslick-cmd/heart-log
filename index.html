Before bed

  1. Force-quit the WHOOP app and turn its Bluetooth off.
  2. On iPhone, set Settings, Display & Brightness, Auto-Lock to Never, and plug in the phone. Set it back in the morning.
  3. Put the phone close to your bed, with Bluefy open on this page.
  4. Tap the band to wake it, tap Connect, and choose your WHOOP.
  5. Wait for a heart rate, then tap Dim screen.
